Sanskrit Term Analysis

Hṛdaya

हृदय • hṛ-da-ya
हृ हरणे, दा दाने, यङ् यमने।
"Hri = to receive; Da = to give; Ya = to control or move."

In classical Upanishadic and Ayurvedic texts, the word Hridaya is not just a label but an explanation of physiological function. The word is composed of three syllables that perfectly describe the cyclic behavior of the heart.

Cardiovascular Link

This 3-syllable etymology maps exactly to the mechanical cardiac cycle: Venous Return ('Hri' - to receive blood), Cardiac Output ('Da' - to eject blood to the body), and Systemic Control/Regulation ('Ya' - the autonomic nervous system control over heart rate and pressure).
